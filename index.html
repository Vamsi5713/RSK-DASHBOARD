e-Panta Checker

Pick a base season and a compare season from the e-Panta data you have already uploaded. The checker compares them survey number by survey number and shows whether the same farmer is still cultivating or a different farmer has taken over, so you can verify on the ground before booking. A farmer counts as "matched" only when both the Khatha number and the Aadhaar (last 4 digits) are identical in the two years; if either differs or is missing, the record is flagged. Only the last 4 Aadhaar digits are kept in the browser.
